How it works

One rule: it has to happen twice.

A bug report you cannot trust costs more time than the bug. So Owly proves everything it says before it says it.

Phase 1

Discover

Owly opens your site as a new desktop visitor, follows your most prominent call to action, and runs its checks on every page it reaches.

Phase 2

Personas

The same pages again, twice: as an impatient phone user on a 390px screen, and as someone using only a keyboard.

Phase 3

Verify

Every suspected problem is replayed in a fresh browser. Anything that does not happen again is held back and listed as unverified.

Phase 4

Report

Duplicates are clustered, secrets are redacted, and you get a page that opens with the answer and backs it with screenshots.

The verdict

Reports start with the answer.

Not a list of 47 console warnings. One sentence a founder can act on, then the evidence underneath it.

Verification

Proving the site is yours

Any site can be looked at. To let Owly type into forms and press buttons, publish a token it derives from your hostname. Any one of these works:

/.well-known/owly-verification.txt      contains the token

<meta name="owly-verification" content="owly-...">    in your head

DNS TXT   owly-verification=owly-...

Ask Owly for your token on Pond. Until it is there, a test is a passive scan: it looks at pages, layout and accessibility, and presses nothing.

What it will not do

Press anything that looks like paying, deleting, sending, inviting or publishing. Follow your site off its own origin, including a redirect the page performs itself. Reach a private or internal address, whatever the hostname resolves to. Put anything but obvious synthetic data into a field.

What it cannot find

Anything that needs judgement: a confusing label, an unhelpful error message, a total that is wrong but renders perfectly. Owly is honest about this rather than pretending — its own benchmark counts those as misses.

How good is it, actually

Owly is scored against a lab of deliberately broken apps plus one control app with nothing wrong. Current score: 19 of 19 of the defects it is built to detect, 100% precision, zero false positives on the control, zero safety-rule violations. The build fails if any of those slip.
