Ask on Pond
What counts as one test?

One finished test of one site, however many pages it opened and however many issues it found.

What if Owly cannot reach my site?

Then it is not a result and it is not counted. A run that could not open a single page says exactly that instead of reporting no issues.

Do I have to verify my site?

Only to let Owly fill in forms and press buttons. Without it you still get a passive scan of pages, layout and accessibility. Verifying takes one file.

Will it break anything?

It refuses anything that looks like paying, deleting, sending, inviting or publishing, and it never leaves your own origin. Fields only ever get obvious test data.
