What counts as one test?

One finished test of one site — however many pages it opened, however many issues it found.

What happens when I run out?

Owly tells you, and stops. Nothing is charged without you choosing it, nothing auto-upgrades, and no test is started that you cannot see the result of. The free tests reset on the first of the month; the 3 a day on this website reset at midnight UTC.

Why is the website capped at 3 a day?

The box on the front page is open to anyone with no account at all, and every test is a real browser on a real server. Three a visitor keeps it open to everybody rather than to whoever scripts it first. Your own five a month are separate, and live on Pond.

What if Owly cannot reach my site?

Then it is not a result and it is not counted. A run that could not open a single page says exactly that, rather than reporting no issues.

Do I have to verify my site?

Only to let Owly fill in forms and press buttons. Without it you still get a passive scan of pages, layout and accessibility. Verifying takes one file.

Will it break anything?

It refuses anything that looks like paying, deleting, sending, inviting or publishing, and it never leaves your own origin. Fields only ever get obvious test data.
