How it works

Three steps. No setup.

01

Give it a link

Ask Owly on Pond to test your site. No script on your pages, no SDK, no account to create on our side.

02

It uses your product

As a new visitor, as an impatient phone user, and as someone with only a keyboard — in a real browser, following your own call to action.

03

You get the answer

One sentence, the failing step, the screen, the failing request, and an issue you can paste straight into GitHub.

Safety

A tester loose in your product is only useful if you can predict it.

never presses

Pay, delete, send, invite, publish, unsubscribe. It stops at the button and says so in the report.

never leaves

Your origin is the whole world. Not by link, not by redirect, not by anything the page attempts on its own.

never types

Anything but obvious test data. Every field gets something like owly.qa+8mpk@example.com.

never assumes

Until you publish a token proving the site is yours, Owly only looks. It will not fill anything in or press anything.
