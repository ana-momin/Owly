What it checks

The things a new user hits in the first five minutes.

The main journey, first

Follows your most prominent call to action end to end and says plainly whether a newcomer got through, with a screenshot of every step.

Forms that fail

Fills them in with obvious test data. Catches server errors, silent failures, and forms that accept an invalid email.

Broken on phones

Sideways scrolling, text that gets cut off, and buttons that overlap or sit under a banner.

Keyboard and accessibility

Invisible focus, dialogs you can't leave with a keyboard, and an automated WCAG 2.1 A/AA scan.

Errors in the page

JavaScript exceptions and console errors, with the line of code that threw them.

Broken links and images

Every link Owly follows, reported once with every page that points to it.

Slow and flaky requests

Calls that take too long, and saves that work twice and fail the third time.

How it works

Ask in chat. Get a report you can hand to a developer.

Give Owly your URL

On Pond, ask it to test your site. Say "just mobile" or "just the forms" to narrow it down.

It uses your site

It crawls your pages as three kinds of user, then replays every suspected problem in a fresh browser.

You get the proof

Steps to reproduce, expected vs actual, the failing request or error, and a one-click GitHub issue.

Straight answers

What it will never do, and what it can't find.

Never

  • Press anything that pays, deletes, sends or invites
  • Follow your site to another website, or into a private network
  • Enter anything but obvious test data, like owly.qa@example.com
  • Fill in forms on a site whose owner hasn't verified it

Can't find yet

  • A label that is confusing but works
  • An error message that is technically there but unhelpful
  • A number that is wrong but still displays
  • Anything behind a login, for now

Verification

Full tests only for sites you control.

Any site gets a passive scan: Owly looks, but doesn't click or type. To let it fill in forms and press buttons, ask Owly on Pond to verify your site. It gives you a token to put in any one of these places:

A file/.well-known/owly-verification.txt
A meta tag<meta name="owly-verification" content="owly-…">
A DNS recordTXT owly-verification=owly-…
Owly: rule-based checks and scripted users in a real browser. Reports are kept for 30 days.
